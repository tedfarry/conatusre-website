## The Problem

An investment group wanted to enter several Midwest markets but lacked the local
infrastructure and deal flow to compete with established buyers. Traditional approaches —
broker relationships and MLS monitoring — were producing insufficient volume and quality.

The group needed a systematic approach that could scale across markets without
proportionally scaling headcount.

## The Process

We designed and implemented a multi-channel acquisition pipeline:

1. **Data infrastructure** — Built automated feeds from public records, MLS, and
   off-market data sources, normalized into a unified property database.
2. **Screening automation** — Developed scoring models that evaluated properties
   against the investment thesis, automatically flagging high-potential targets.
3. **Underwriting templates** — Created standardized underwriting models that
   pulled in market data automatically, reducing analysis time per property.
4. **Outreach automation** — Built direct-to-seller outreach workflows for
   off-market opportunities identified through data analysis.

Each component was designed to operate independently but integrate into a
cohesive pipeline from lead to close.

## The Outcome

Over the first quarter of operation:

- **Deal flow tripled** compared to the previous broker-only approach.
- **Analysis time per property dropped significantly** thanks to automated
  data population and standardized underwriting.
- **Off-market deal percentage increased** as the direct outreach workflows
  generated opportunities invisible to the broader market.
- **Market entry timeline compressed** — the group went from zero presence
  to active pipeline in multiple markets simultaneously.
