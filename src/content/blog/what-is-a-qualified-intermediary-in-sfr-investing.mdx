A Qualified Intermediary (QI) is an unrelated party who facilitates tax-deferred 1031 exchanges. Also called Accommodators or Facilitators, QIs participate in the disposition of relinquished property and acquisition of replacement property under IRC Section 1031, holding no economic interest except their fee.
