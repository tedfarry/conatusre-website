Rehabilitation encompasses the necessary repairs to prepare investment properties for tenants. The scope varies significantly — minor updates like paint and lighting upgrades coexist with substantial work including roof replacement and plumbing system overhauls. Properties receive inspections before purchase to identify repair needs.
