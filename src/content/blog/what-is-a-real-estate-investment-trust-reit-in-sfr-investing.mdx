A Real Estate Investment Trust (REIT) is an entity that owns and operates income-generating properties including offices, shopping centers, apartments, hotels, storage facilities, warehouses, single-family rentals, and real estate loans. REITs allow individual investors to earn income shares without direct property ownership.
