Conatus founders Ted Farry and Torey Severino discuss why California real estate owners should consider trading property for Single Family Rental portfolios elsewhere. The platform helps owners with large gains to monetize the equity in their property without paying capital gains taxes, often achieving 2 to 3 times their current income.

The discussion explores geographic arbitrage benefits between high-cost California markets and lower-cost rental markets like San Antonio, Texas, where investors can achieve significantly higher yields while deferring all capital gains through 1031 exchanges.
