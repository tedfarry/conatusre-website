A self-directed IRA (SDIRA) is an Individual Retirement Account offering specified tax treatment with expanded investment flexibility. SDIRAs provide investors opportunities for asset diversification beyond traditional stocks, bonds, and mutual funds.

These accounts can invest in single family rental properties, alternative real estate types, private market securities, and other assets. All investments are held in accounts administered by a custodian or trustee.
