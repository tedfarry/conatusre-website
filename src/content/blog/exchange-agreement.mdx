An Exchange Agreement is a written agreement between the Qualified Intermediary (QI) and Exchangor setting forth the Exchangor's intent to exchange relinquished property for replacement property. It outlines mutual obligations, conditions, and procedural requirements governing the tax-deferred transaction.
