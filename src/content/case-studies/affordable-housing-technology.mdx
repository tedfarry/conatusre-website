## The Problem

Managing a scattered-site SFR portfolio leased to Housing Choice Voucher (HCV)
tenants involves layers of operational complexity that generic property management
software ignores. HCV lease-ups require coordination with local housing authorities,
compliance with inspection requirements, rent reasonableness documentation,
HAP contract management, and tenant communication workflows that differ
fundamentally from market-rate operations.

Off-the-shelf tools treated HCV as an afterthought. The operation needed a
platform built from the ground up for this specific use case.

## The Process

We designed and built a fully integrated asset management platform purpose-built
for HCV operations:

1. **Housing authority integration** — Structured workflows for each stage of the
   HCV lease-up process, from initial voucher verification through HAP contract
   execution, with automated status tracking per housing authority.
2. **Inspection management** — Automated scheduling, preparation checklists,
   and follow-up workflows for HQS inspections, with historical tracking
   to identify recurring issues by property.
3. **AI-powered document processing** — Claude-integrated workflows that process
   incoming housing authority correspondence, extract key data points, and
   route actions to the appropriate team members automatically.
4. **Tenant communication system** — Purpose-built communication tools that
   account for the specific needs and timelines of HCV tenants and the
   housing authorities they work with.
5. **Compliance dashboard** — Real-time visibility into portfolio-wide compliance
   status, upcoming deadlines, and pending actions across all housing authorities.

## The Outcome

The platform transformed what had been a manual, error-prone operation into
a systematic, scalable process:

- **Lease-up timelines shortened** as automated workflows eliminated coordination
  delays between the team, tenants, and housing authorities.
- **Compliance visibility became real-time** — the team could see exactly where
  every unit stood in the HCV process at any moment.
- **AI document processing reduced manual data entry** and accelerated response
  times to housing authority communications.
- **The system scaled** to accommodate portfolio growth without proportional
  increases in operational staff.
