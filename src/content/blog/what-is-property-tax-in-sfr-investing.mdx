Property tax is a real estate ad-valorem tax, calculated by a local government, which is paid by the owner based on assessed property value. Property taxes fund municipal services like water, sewer, law enforcement, and fire services. States offer various homestead exemptions, but investors may not qualify and could face higher rates upon purchase.
