The one percent rule refers to the rent-to-cost ratio a single family rental investment requires for profitability. Monthly rent should equal at least 1% of the total purchase price for positive returns.

The "2% rule" gained popularity during market downturns when distressed properties could be acquired at deep discounts, allowing for higher rent-to-price ratios.
