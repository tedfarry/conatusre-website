Join Conatus Real Estate, Udirect IRA and Andrew Karigan – Douglas Elliman for an investor networking event on October 18th, 2018 in Laguna Beach. The event was held at a gorgeous listing of Andrew Karigan and showcased a special string quartet performance with musicians Robert Schumitzky, Madalyn Parnas, Adam Neeley and Erin Breene who perform regularly with the Pacific Symphony, San Diego Symphony, and the Hollywood Bowl Orchestra.

Conatus Real Estate is a Single Family Rental Investment Platform. We help California real estate owners with large gains to monetize the equity in their property without paying capital gains taxes. Many times we are able to help our customers achieve 2 to 3 times their current income.
