An Accommodator serves as an intermediary in Section 1031 tax-deferred exchanges. This party holds funds in escrow and receives only a fee for facilitating the transaction. The role is technically termed Qualified Intermediary (QI) but carries several names including Accommodator, Intermediary, or Facilitator.
