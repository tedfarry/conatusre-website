A Home Inspection is an examination of a property's condition, typically performed in connection with the property's sale. Inspectors evaluate structural and mechanical systems including roofing, plumbing, electrical infrastructure, foundations, and climate control systems, as well as water damage, pest infestation, and fire safety concerns.
