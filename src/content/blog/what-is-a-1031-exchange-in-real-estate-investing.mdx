A 1031 exchange is a tax-deferred transaction under Section 1031 of the Internal Revenue Code. It involves exchanging a relinquished property for like-kind replacement property to defer federal and state capital gains and depreciation recapture taxes.

This strategy enables investors to reinvest all proceeds without immediate tax liability, effectively trading equity for increased income potential.
