## Beyond the Hype

AI in real estate has been heavily marketed but unevenly adopted. The gap between
what's promised and what's practical remains wide. Our approach focuses on specific,
measurable workflow improvements rather than broad AI transformation narratives.

## Where AI Adds Real Value

After deploying AI workflows across multiple consulting engagements, we've found
the highest-value applications are:

- **Research aggregation** — Pulling and synthesizing data from multiple sources
  into structured reports that would take hours manually.
- **Document analysis** — Extracting key terms and risks from leases, inspections,
  and title reports.
- **Comparable analysis** — Automated comp pulls with quality scoring that flags
  outliers and adjustments.

## The Human-in-the-Loop Principle

Every AI workflow we build maintains human oversight on critical decisions.
The AI handles research, aggregation, and preliminary analysis. Humans make
the final calls on pricing, acquisitions, and strategy.

This isn't a limitation — it's a feature. The best outcomes come from combining
AI speed with human judgment.
