## The Problem

Scaling an SFR acquisitions operation beyond a handful of markets requires systematizing
every step from lead identification to offer execution. Manual processes — monitoring
MLS feeds, pulling comps by hand, building underwriting models in spreadsheets,
and submitting offers through broker portals — create bottlenecks that limit
throughput and introduce human error at every stage.

The challenge: build a platform that could handle high-volume deal flow across
multiple markets while maintaining disciplined underwriting standards.

## The Process

We designed and built a proprietary acquisitions platform from the ground up:

1. **Live lead ingestion** — Automated feeds from public records, MLS, and off-market
   data sources, normalized into a unified property database with real-time updates.
2. **Systematic data enrichment** — Every incoming record is automatically enriched
   with tax data, comparable sales, rental estimates, ownership history, and
   neighborhood analytics before an underwriter ever sees it.
3. **Integrated underwriting environment** — Underwriters work inside the platform
   with all relevant data pre-populated. Standardized models enforce consistent
   criteria while allowing market-specific adjustments.
4. **Automated offer submission** — Once an underwriter approves a deal, offers
   are submitted automatically with the push of a button — formatted correctly
   for the target channel, with all required documentation attached.

## The Outcome

The platform became the operational backbone of a high-volume acquisitions operation:

- **3,000+ SFR transactions executed** through the platform across multiple markets.
- **Over $1B in acquisitions capital** deployed through systematic, data-driven processes.
- **Underwriting throughput increased dramatically** as manual data gathering was
  eliminated and underwriters focused purely on analysis and decision-making.
- **Offer velocity improved** — the time from lead identification to submitted offer
  compressed from days to hours.
- **Consistent underwriting standards** maintained across all markets through
  platform-enforced criteria and automated quality checks.
