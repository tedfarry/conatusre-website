Accelerated depreciation is a method allowing investors to deduct a greater portion of depreciable property costs during the early years of ownership. This frontloads deductions rather than spreading costs evenly across the asset's useful life.

Cost segregation analysis and professional tax guidance are recommended for proper implementation of accelerated depreciation strategies.
