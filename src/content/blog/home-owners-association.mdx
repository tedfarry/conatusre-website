A Home Owners Association (HOA) is an organization in a subdivision, planned community, or condominium that makes and enforces rules for the properties in its jurisdiction. Properties within these associations typically require monthly, quarterly, or annual fees funding common area maintenance. Investors should verify whether an HOA permits rental properties before purchasing.
