A Starker Exchange is an alternative name for tax-deferred, like-kind exchange transactions, named after the 1979 court decision Starker vs. Commissioner. The Ninth Circuit Court of Appeals validated that delayed tax-deferred, like-kind exchanges constitute valid exchanges under IRC Section 1031.

This ruling established the precedent for modern delayed exchange structures used in real estate investing today.
