title: "What Is A Leasing Fee (Commission) In SFR Investing?"
description: "Leasing fees are compensation paid to agents or property managers for securing new tenant leases and renewals."
pubDate: 2018-11-08

Leasing fees are paid when agents successfully sign leases with new tenants on single-family rental properties. Re-leasing fees typically apply when existing tenants renew their lease agreements, representing operational costs that impact investment returns.
