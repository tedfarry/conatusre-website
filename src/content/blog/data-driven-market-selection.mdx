## Beyond the Hottest Market List

Every real estate publication runs their annual "best markets" list. The problem?
These lists optimize for clicks, not for your specific investment thesis.

A systematic approach to market selection starts with defining your criteria,
then letting the data surface opportunities.

## The Framework

Our market evaluation framework considers five dimensions:

1. **Economic fundamentals** — Job growth, industry diversification, wage trends
2. **Supply dynamics** — Permitting trends, construction pipeline, inventory levels
3. **Rental demand** — Population growth, rent-to-income ratios, vacancy rates
4. **Regulatory environment** — Landlord-tenant laws, tax structure, zoning
5. **Operational feasibility** — Vendor availability, management infrastructure, proximity

Each dimension is scored and weighted based on the investor's specific strategy
and constraints.

## Putting It Into Practice

The goal isn't to find the single "best" market — it's to build a shortlist of
markets that align with your investment thesis and operational capabilities.
From there, boots-on-the-ground research fills in the nuances that data alone can't capture.
