An intermediary in SFR investing serves as a neutral third party in tax-deferred 1031 exchanges. This role involves facilitating the disposition of the Exchangor's relinquished property and the acquisition of replacement property. Also known as a Qualified Intermediary (QI), Accommodator, or Facilitator, the intermediary has no financial stake except for compensation received.
