A trust establishes a fiduciary relationship where a trustor authorizes a trustee to hold title to single-family rental properties or other assets benefiting a designated beneficiary.

Trusts provide legal asset protection, ensure distribution according to trustor wishes, reduce paperwork and timelines, and may minimize inheritance or estate tax obligations. In finance, trusts can also function as public limited companies, including Delaware Statutory Trusts or Real Estate Investment Trusts.
