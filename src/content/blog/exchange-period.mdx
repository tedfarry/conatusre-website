title: "What Is Exchange Period In SFR Investing?"

pubDate: 2018-11-05

tags: ["exchange period", "1031", "1031 exchange", "conatus", "conatus real estate", "real estate investing", "real estate investment", "single family rental"]

The Exchange Period spans 180 calendar days from the first property transfer or the exchangor's tax return due date (whichever arrives first). A 45-day identification period precedes this window, requiring exchangors to designate replacement properties within that earlier timeframe before acquisition must commence.
