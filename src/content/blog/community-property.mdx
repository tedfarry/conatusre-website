Community property refers to assets acquired during marriage where each spouse holds equal ownership rights. Gifts and inheritances remain separate property. This concept applies in nine states: Arizona, California, Idaho, Louisiana, Nevada, New Mexico, Texas, Washington, and Wisconsin.
